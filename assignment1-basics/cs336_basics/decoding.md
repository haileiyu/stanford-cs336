Once upon a time, in a small village, there was a little girl named Lily. She was very happy and loved to play outside. One day, she saw a big, impressive tree with a hole in it. She wondered what was inside the hole.
Lily went to the tree and saw a little bird. The bird said, "Hi, Lily! What are you doing in the tree?" Lily said, "I want to see what is inside the tree." The bird said, "Be careful, don't go near the tree."
Lily went to the tree and saw a tiny, shiny box. She opened the box and found a toy bear. But the bear was not real! It was a toy bear with a missing arm. Lily felt sad and said, "I'm sorry, bear. I didn't mean to make a mess."
The bear smiled and said, "It's okay, Lily. I'm sorry for scaring you." Lily was happy to have a new friend, even though it was a toy. She learned to be more careful when playing near the tree.
